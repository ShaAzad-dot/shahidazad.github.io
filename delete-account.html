How to Request Account Deletion

To delete your account and all associated data from FinTrack, please follow the steps below:

  1. Open the FinTrack app
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Select "Delete Account"
  4. Confirm your request

If you are unable to access the app, you can request deletion by emailing us:

Email: sshahid.fazad@gmail.com

Data Deletion Details

When you request account deletion:

  • Your account information will be permanently deleted
  • Your financial data (loans, expenses, cards) will be removed
  • Your login credentials will be deleted

Some data may be retained temporarily for legal or security purposes.
