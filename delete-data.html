How to Reset Your Data

FinTrack allows users to reset their account data without deleting their account.

  1. Open the FinTrack app
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Select "Reset Account"
  4. Confirm to delete your stored data

What Data is Deleted

  • All financial records (loans, expenses, cards)
  • App usage data

What Data is Kept

  • Your login account (email and password)

For complete account deletion, please refer to the account deletion page.
