FinTrack

Your Ultimate AI-Powered Wealth & Debt Manager

About The App

FinTrack is a complete personal finance ecosystem. Move beyond simple expense tracking and take total control of your bank balances, credit cards, loans, investments, and personal debts. Powered by advanced AI (Claude), FinTrack analyzes your financial behavior to help you clear debts faster and grow your assets.

Core Features

🏦 Accounts & Cards Management

  • Live tracking of Bank Account available balances based on your income and expenses.
  • Credit Card tracking: Monitor total limits, utilized limits, and available limits instantly.

📊 Loans, Debts & Bills

  • Loan Tracking: Track principal amounts, interest rates, paid/pending EMIs, and loan end dates.
  • Personal Debts: Log who you borrowed from (or lent to), expected return dates, and tracking partial payments.
  • Bill Management: Monthly lists of upcoming bills to ensure you never miss a due date.

📈 Assets, Investments & Goals

  • Track real-world assets and log their appreciating or depreciating values.
  • Monitor investments: See where your money is invested and track real-time profit and loss.
  • Set and track dedicated financial goals.

🤖 AI Financial Assistant & Reports

  • AI Chat: Powered by Claude API, our AI analyzes your spending behavior and provides personalized advice to clear debts and optimize savings.
  • Generate and export comprehensive PDF reports of your financial health.
  • Receive timely alerts via Email and WhatsApp messages.
