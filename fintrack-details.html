FinTrack

Take Control of Your Finances

About The App

FinTrack is a comprehensive finance management tool built to help you track expenses, monitor subscriptions, and achieve your financial goals. Built with Flutter for a smooth, native experience.

Features

  • Intuitive expense and income logging.
  • Visual charts and monthly breakdown reports.
  • Secure local data storage (No cloud dependency).
  • Dark mode support.
