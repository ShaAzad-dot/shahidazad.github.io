Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2026

This privacy policy applies to all Android applications developed by Shahid Azad Apps.

1. Information Collection

Our primary goal is to provide a great user experience. Our applications may collect non-personal information such as device types, operating system versions, and crash reports to improve performance and fix bugs.

2. Data Storage

Where applicable, our apps (like FinTrack) store your data locally on your device. We do not transmit your personal financial entries or private data to external servers unless explicitly stated or linked to a backup service you control.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Some applications may use third-party services (such as Google AdMob or Firebase Analytics). These services may collect anonymous data, such as advertising identifiers, to provide relevant ads and analyze app usage. These services operate under their own privacy policies.

4. Data Security

We value your trust and strive to protect your information using commercially acceptable means. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.
